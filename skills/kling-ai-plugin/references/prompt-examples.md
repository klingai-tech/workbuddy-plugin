# Kling AI prompts and usage examples

## Suggested prompts

- Paint a red panda in a vintage spacesuit, floating by a space-station window with Earth's blue glow illuminating its face, rich detail, and cinematic lighting.
- Create a 5-second cinematic video: a mech warrior lands heavily from above, a shockwave scatters rubble and dust, and the camera pushes in rapidly for impact.
- Create a 15-second sports-shoe marketing clip: open on an urban street, cut within three seconds to product detail and dynamic wearing shots, and finish on a close-up of the upper.

## Natural-language requests

Text-to-video:

> Use Kling to create a 5-second, 16:9 cinematic video: a vintage motorcycle slowly stops outside a convenience store on a rainy night as the camera smoothly pushes in from a wide shot.

Image-to-video:

> Animate my attached image for 5 seconds at 720p. Preserve identity, facial features, and clothing; move only the camera slowly from the left side to the front.

Multi-shot video:

> Make a product film: establish the scene in a wide shot, move closer and show the product from the side, then hold on a brand detail. Use smooth transitions and currently supported duration/shot parameters.

Text-to-image:

> Create a 16:9 poster key visual: a transparent glass teapot in a minimal white studio, soft side lighting, and title-safe space on the right.

Complete generation request:

> Make a 5-second, 16:9, 720p continuous shot of a vintage motorcycle slowly stopping outside a convenience store on a rainy night. Return the task number.

Status:

> Check this Kling task number once for its current status, without a polling loop.

Examples do not establish model availability or legal parameter values; validate against the selected live model.

## Prompt construction

Prioritize subject/environment, action/change, camera/shot structure, lighting/style, identity/continuity constraints, and exclusions only when they prevent likely errors. Avoid repetitive negative prompts. For image-to-video, distinguish protected facts from permitted motion.

## User-facing results

Do not add credit warnings or separate confirmation. Disclose billing before submission only when the live tool requires it, using actual returned scope. Acceptance reports a real task number and status. After success, deliver through the shared workflow; do not replace executable querying or delivery with a promise to return later.

Describe only inspected quality. Report actual defects and feasible improvements rather than equating generation success with perfection. Refresh expired links by original task number without regeneration.
